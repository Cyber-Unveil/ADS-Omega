# ADS-Ω™ Control Architecture --- Public Summary

**Source:** ADS-Ω-ARCH-012\
**Publication class:** PUBLIC-SUMMARY

ADS-Ω™ has exactly seven core control layers:

1.  **DCE™** --- decision initialization and identity.
2.  **CIF™** --- context integrity and admissibility.
3.  **RVL™** --- observable decision-basis validation.
4.  **AVC™** --- decision-risk and uncertainty evaluation.
5.  **EG™** --- final authorization and enforcement.
6.  **TMG™** --- evidence continuity and provenance.
7.  **CCF™** --- monitoring, containment, and recovery.

Decision Constraint Envelope, DAS™, DPR™, CIL™, ICI™, DRS™, Decision
Autopsy™, telemetry, dashboards, and external runtime substrates are not
additional core control layers.
