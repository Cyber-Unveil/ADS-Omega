## The premise

> **The decision is the security object.**

Organizations already protect identities, systems, data, models,
applications, and tools. ADS-Ω™ addresses the consequential decision
that connects those assets to real-world action and impact.

**AI / Automation / Human Input → Decision → Authorization → Action →
Impact**

## What ADS-Ω™ is

ADS-Ω™ is a proprietary decision-security framework for keeping
consequential autonomous and AI-assisted decisions bounded,
attributable, reconstructable, and containable.

Its governing principle is:

> **Velocity of execution must not exceed velocity of accountability.**

## Seven core control layers

1.  **DCE™** --- establishes decision identity and initialization.
2.  **CIF™** --- evaluates context integrity, provenance, freshness, and
    admissibility.
3.  **RVL™** --- validates the observable decision basis.
4.  **AVC™** --- evaluates decision risk and uncertainty.
5.  **EG™** --- authorizes and enforces the decision boundary.
6.  **TMG™** --- preserves decision evidence and provenance.
7.  **CCF™** --- monitors material conditions and enforces containment
    and recovery.

ADS-Ω™ has exactly seven core control layers.

## Decision Authorization States

ADS-Ω™ uses five authorization states:

**GRANT · BLOCK · ADJUST · GATE · SUSPEND**

A GRANT is bounded permission, not permanent trust.

## Evidence and accountability

ADS-Ω™ distinguishes context, decision basis, risk, authority,
constraints, authorization, execution, containment, and outcome.
Evidence may be minimized or retained through protected references
rather than indiscriminately embedding sensitive information.

## What ADS-Ω™ is not

ADS-Ω™ is not represented as an international standard, regulatory
mandate, certification, guarantee of correct decisions, or industry-wide
accepted practice. It does not replace cybersecurity, AI governance,
enterprise risk management, legal analysis, or accountable human
judgment.

## AARM

Current ADS-Ω™ engineering may use AARM as an external runtime substrate
direction. AARM is third-party, is not an ADS-Ω™ core control layer, and
does not own ADS-Ω™ doctrine or authorization semantics. Capability
mappings require evidence.
