# ADS-Ω™ Enterprise Architecture --- Public Summary

**Source:** ADS-Ω-ARCH-010\
**Publication class:** PUBLIC-SUMMARY

ADS-Ω™ organizes decision security across Constitution/Governance,
Architecture, Runtime, Assurance, and controlled public derivatives.

At the decision-security plane, DCE™, CIF™, RVL™, AVC™, and EG™
progressively establish and evaluate a decision before consequential
effect. TMG™ preserves evidence continuity across the lifecycle. CCF™
monitors material conditions and enforces containment when required.

Models, agents, tools, identity systems, policy engines, target systems,
evidence stores, human approvers, and external runtime substrates remain
connected systems rather than additional ADS-Ω™ core layers.

The architecture preserves four invariants: exactly seven core layers; a
Decision Trust Boundary; bounded authorization through a Decision
Constraint Envelope; and attributable evidence supporting
reconstruction.
