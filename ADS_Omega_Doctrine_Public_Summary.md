## Core doctrine

-   **The decision is the security object.**
-   **AI → Decision → Impact.**
-   **Velocity of execution must not exceed velocity of
    accountability.**
-   Zero Trust principles apply to consequential decisions.
-   Human accountability remains attributable.
-   Confidence is not evidence.
-   The record is not automatically the truth.
-   Technical capability does not equal decision authority.
-   Authorization, execution, and outcome are distinct.

## Structural invariant

ADS-Ω™ contains exactly seven core control layers:

**DCE™ · CIF™ · RVL™ · AVC™ · EG™ · TMG™ · CCF™**

Subordinate constructs do not create additional core layers.

## Decision-security principle

Consequential external action should occur only after applicable
decision identity, context, decision-basis, risk, authority, constraint,
and authorization conditions are satisfied.

Unknown, error, timeout, or mandatory dependency failure is not
automatically permission.

## Evidence and privacy

Defensible decision records require attributable evidence, but
accountability does not require indiscriminate retention of raw
sensitive information. Evidence may be minimized or protected through
appropriate references and transformations.

## Containment

Authorization is bounded. Material changes can invalidate prior
permission. Containment requires actual restriction, not merely an
alert.
