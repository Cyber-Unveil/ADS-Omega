# ADS-Ω™ Decision Trust Boundary --- Public Summary

**Source:** ADS-Ω-ARCH-013\
**Publication class:** PUBLIC-SUMMARY

The Decision Trust Boundary separates a proposed consequential action
from an authorized external effect.

Before a decision crosses that boundary, applicable decision identity,
context, observable decision-basis, risk, authority, constraint, and
authorization requirements must be satisfied.

Execution Governance binds authorization to the specific decision and
its permitted action, target, tool, policy, authority, constraints, and
validity.

A prior GRANT may become invalid after material change, expiry,
revocation, containment, evidence-integrity failure, or mandatory
dependency failure.

**Unknown, error, or timeout is not GRANT.**
