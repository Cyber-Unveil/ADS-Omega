# ADS-Ω™ Selected Canonical Definitions --- Public Export

**Source:** ADS-Ω-CONST-002\
**Publication class:** PUBLIC-SUMMARY\
**Authority:** Selected public export only. ADS-Ω-CONST-002 in the
frozen internal baseline is the authoritative definition source.

## Selected definitions

**ADS-Ω™** --- Proprietary decision-security framework developed by
Cyber Unveil International, LLC.

**Decision** --- Governed security object representing a consequential
determination capable of producing external effect.

**Consequential Decision** --- Decision capable of materially affecting
people, assets, rights, finances, operations, safety, resilience,
compliance, or enterprise exposure.

**Decision Identifier** --- Unique identifier assigned to a governed
decision instance.

**Decision Trust Boundary** --- Architecture boundary separating
proposed consequential action from authorized external effect.

**Decision Constraint Envelope** --- Effective set of constraints
governing the permitted scope of a decision and its execution.

**DCE™** --- Decision Core Engine; initializes decision identity, class,
source, authority context, and policy binding.

**CIF™** --- Context Integrity Field; evaluates context provenance,
freshness, integrity, admissibility, conflict, and trust.

**RVL™** --- Reasoning Validation Layer; evaluates observable
decision-basis artifacts for context, policy, intent, and reasoning
consistency.

**Reasoning Artifact** --- Observable structured artifact representing
the decision basis without requiring private model chain-of-thought.

**AVC™** --- Action Vector Control; evaluates decision risk,
uncertainty, thresholds, cumulative exposure, and authority
requirements.

**EG™** --- Execution Governance; owns final authorization and Decision
Trust Boundary enforcement.

**DAS™** --- Decision Authorization State construct used by Execution
Governance.

**GRANT** --- Bounded permission to execute within approved decision,
scope, constraints, and validity.

**BLOCK** --- Authorization state prohibiting execution.

**ADJUST** --- Authorization state permitting only an explicitly
authorized modified action.

**GATE** --- Non-executing authorization state requiring specified human
or external authority.

**SUSPEND** --- Non-executing authorization state holding activity
pending resolution or reevaluation.

**TMG™** --- Trace Matrix Grid; owns runtime evidence continuity and
DPR™ assembly where required.

**DPR™** --- Decision Provenance Receipt; normalized evidence package
for reconstructing a decision lifecycle.

**CCF™** --- Cognitive Containment Field; owns monitoring, containment
trigger evaluation, restriction, recovery, and authorized exit.

**Containment** --- Actual restriction of affected decision capability
or scope; not merely alerting.

**Context Trust** --- Assessment of provenance, integrity, freshness,
and admissibility; not equivalent to truth.

**Decision Basis** --- Observable information, rules, rationale
artifacts, policies, and computational results used to support a
decision.

**Authority** --- Governed right to approve, constrain, execute, accept,
or disposition a decision or framework change.

**Technical Capability** --- What a system can technically perform; not
equivalent to authority.

**Executed Action** --- Action actually performed by the target or tool.

**Observed Outcome** --- Observed consequence after execution.

**Evidence Gap** --- Missing or insufficient evidence relevant to a
required claim; not automatically a control failure.

**Decision Autopsy™** --- Assurance methodology for reconstructing a
consequential decision lifecycle from attributable evidence.
